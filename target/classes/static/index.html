Our Story

Pioneering Technology Excellence in Cameroon

World Advanced Technology (WAT) is Cameroon's premier IT solutions company, founded with a singular vision: to bring enterprise-grade technology to African businesses at accessible prices.

Led by CEO NGWA CLOVIS OCHANG, a Cisco-certified network engineer and AWS Cloud professional, our team delivers cutting-edge DevOps infrastructure, private VoIP telephone systems, and comprehensive IT consultancy.

Our VoIP solutions are trusted by banks, microfinances and multi-branch companies, enabling seamless, cost-effective communication nationally and internationally.
